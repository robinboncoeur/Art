# No Room

[ *Charlie* ]

They were arguing again. It seemed like every time they talked these days, they would argue.

I'd gotten home a bit early that evening. Mum was in the kitchen, fixing dinner and talking to dad on speakerphone.

"Why are you being like this?" I heard her say.

I heard my Dad's voice then. It had that intense pitch he got when he was furious.

"You're telling me that he just walked away from a decent job to, what, sew dresses?"

"Roger!"

I'd heard enough.

An all-too-familiar knot gripped my throat as I entered my room. 

Suddenly, the phone went silent.

"Charles?"

I stared at nothing.

"Yes, Mum?"

She appeared at my door, her face drawn. Tired. She tipped her head.

"Good day?"

I nodded. Then, stopped for a beat.

"I heard you talking."

She sat down beside me on the bed.

"Your dad—"

"Yeah. I know." I grimaced. "I— I can't face him, Mum. I can't deal with it."

"He'll get over it, hun," she said. 

Both of us knew better.

This would be served at tea, along with the broccoli and peas.

"I've decided to move out, Mum."

Her eyebrows rose.

"Where to?"

"No idea."

We sat at the dinner table in the too-big dining room. The tightness in my throat was still there.

"Sometimes it's best to face things," Mum said.

I looked over at her and shook my head.

"This is not one of those times."

Her lips went thin.

"We'll see what we can do."

"Thanks, Mum."

Mrs Harwood was one of Mum's patients. She had a place near the edge of town. Mrs Harwood's family had owned most of the land in the area years ago, but most of it had been sold to cover debts. She now lived on a small two-horse 'ranch', with an unsealed driveway and paint peeling off the front door.

Her sheepdog barked once as we entered then decided he'd done his bit and hobbled back under the kitchen table.

We sat down on creaky chairs. Mrs Harwood gave me a "once-over".

"This is— Charles?"

I felt my face heat up. Old Harwood was giving me the 'Graham treatment': "Not fit for purpose."

We didn't stay long. 

No point.

When I did a search on the "Flatmates" site for accommodations in Anglesea, I got a '404: page not found'. Same for Jan Juc and Torquay.
