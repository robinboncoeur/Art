## ...How To Be

[ Lauren ]

When I stepped into Celeste and Charlotte's apartment, I noticed there was nowhere to put my keys. There was a spot for Charlotte's shoes, and Celeste's coat, but no place for keys.

Perhaps they just kept them in their purses. Even though Charlotte didn't drive, surely she held a set of keys when she had to walk home from the bus.

Surely.
