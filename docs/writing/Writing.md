## A Mistaken Noun


[![The Writer][St00]{ .artR width="460" }][St00]


[ *Sarah* ]

When Lauren's keys hit the bowl at the entryway, I felt far too much relief. And, annoyed at myself for feeling relieved. Lauren's a grown woman, I sternly told myself, she can take care of herself.

And then, I felt her kiss on my neck and told myself to sod off.

"Sorry I'm a bit late, got to talking to Celeste," she said.

"Oh? About what?"

"This and that," she said — evasively, I thought. "I don't think we're going to see much of her for the next little while."

My phone told me it was time to give the sausages a turn. The fry-pan sizzle made it hard to hear what Lauren was saying.

"Hang on a second, sweetie," I said. "Hope you're okay with garlic mash."

"Sounds lovely!" 

From the kitchen I watched her remove her shoes. Mum shoes, she called them. Paired with mum jeans — or "Mom-jeans", as the Yanks would say — she was still somewhere between netball tournaments and teacher-association meetings and direct-to-boot stores pickups. She caught me looking and flashed me a smile.

She could still make my heart skip a beat.

I was just looking at the last mouthfuls of mash and thinking I'd served myself too much again when she said:

"That was addictive, sugar!"

My quick smile — too quick and short — produced raised brows.

"Something on your mind?"

"It's just Charlotte," I said carefully. "
