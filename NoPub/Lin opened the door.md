Lin opened the door. The Chinese SUV hadn't been hers long and it already irrtated her.

She opened the boot. The new-car smell had aready gone: leaving the window open last night during the storm hadn't helped.

The sun burnt her neck as she pulled the grocery bag towards her. A lemon pushed its way out of a small tear.

Then, the paper bag began to tear.

Lin swore.

"By all that is pure in the universe!" A crow laughed from the eucalipt tree.

Lin bit her lip. Put her hand on the warm yellow paint of the back of the SUV. Sighed.

"Why is Elgar playing in my head," she said. The crow laughed again.

The raw bricks on the driveway burnt her feet through the thin soles of her shoes. She scooped up the rest of the groceries as best she could and slammed the boot. Fumbled in her bag for her house keys.

The crow laughed again.

Cool air and a remnant aura of last night's spag bol greeted her as she swung the door open. The paper bag gave up as she dropped it on the one clear spot on an overworked kitchen bench. Lemons rolled everywhere. Her shoulders sagged.

'Where was Eddie?'
